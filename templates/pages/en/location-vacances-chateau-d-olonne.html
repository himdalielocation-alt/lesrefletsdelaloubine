Château-d'Olonne: the wild coast of Les Sables-d'Olonne

South of Les Sables-d'Olonne, Château-d'Olonne shows a wilder, quieter side of the resort: cliffs, coves and a coastal path. This is where you will find our Les Reflets de la Loubine apartment, in the Lagrange L'Estran residence, in the La Pironnière neighbourhood.

Château-d'Olonne, now part of Les Sables-d'Olonne

Formerly a town in its own right, Château-d'Olonne merged on 1 January 2019 with Olonne-sur-Mer and Les Sables-d'Olonne to form the new town of Les Sables-d'Olonne. The name lives on, on road signs and in addresses: our apartment is at "Château-d'Olonne, 85180 Les Sables-d'Olonne".

The La Pironnière neighbourhood, laid out by the ocean in the late 1950s, faces the sea and the rocky coast, away from the bustle of the centre.

The wild coast and the Puits d'Enfer

The Château-d'Olonne shoreline is made of rocks and low cliffs. Its most famous site, the Puits d'Enfer, is a chasm into which the ocean surges with a roar: it lies right opposite our residence.

The coastal path lets you follow this coast on foot, from one viewpoint to the next.

Cayola, Tanchet and the beaches

Between coves and sandy beaches, the area offers plenty of variety: La Pironnière beach, the nearest, Cayola cove, surrounded by rocks, and Tanchet beach, about 1.5 km away, with lifeguarded swimming in season. Details in our beach guide.

Heritage and nature

Château-d'Olonne is home to the Romanesque Abbey of Saint-Jean-d'Orbestier, a reminder of the area's rich past. For nature, Lake Tanchet and the Zoo des Sables make great family outings, and the cycle paths link the neighbourhood to the rest of the coast.
