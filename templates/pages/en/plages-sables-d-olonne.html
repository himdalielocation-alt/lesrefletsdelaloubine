The beaches around La Pironnière: our guide

From our Les Reflets de la Loubine apartment, in the Lagrange L'Estran residence in Château-d'Olonne, you can choose between rocky coves, family beaches and long stretches of sand. Here are the beaches we recommend, from the nearest to the furthest.

{{PHOTO:act-plages}}

La Pironnière beach: the nearest

This is the closest beach to the residence. Family-friendly and sheltered, it is perfect early in the morning, when the tide uncovers the rocks: children love hunting for crabs and shells.

Cayola cove: the photogenic inlet

A little further on, Cayola cove is a small inlet surrounded by rocks. It is one of the most photogenic spots on the coast, especially at sunset. With all the rocks, good shoes come in handy to explore it.

It lies on the wild coast, like the Puits d'Enfer, which you can see on the same walk.

Tanchet beach: fine sand and lifeguarded swimming

{{PHOTO:plage}}

About 1.5 km from the apartment, Tanchet beach is a lovely sandy beach with lifeguarded swimming in season. A safe bet for a family beach day, not far from Lake Tanchet and the zoo.

The Grande Plage: seaside-resort atmosphere

About 4 km away, in the heart of Les Sables-d'Olonne, the Grande Plage stretches along the Remblai promenade. Its kilometres of fine sand, calm water and lifeguarded swimming in season make it the family beach par excellence, with restaurants and shops close by.

Sauveterre and Le Veillon: the wild beaches

To the north, Sauveterre beach, in Olonne-sur-Mer, is lined with dunes and the state forest. Wilder and with few facilities, it is popular with surfers and nature lovers, and lifeguarded in summer.

To the south, about 9 km away, Le Veillon beach in Talmont-Saint-Hilaire is a large, more unspoilt beach, ideal for getting away from the crowds.

Practical tips

Lifeguards: the Grande Plage and Tanchet beach are usually lifeguarded at weekends from spring, then every day in summer; the other lifeguarded beaches are covered from June. Dates and hours are set each year by the town of Les Sables-d'Olonne: check them on site and always follow the flags.

Tides: the tidal range on the Vendée coast is large. Check the tide times, especially for rock-pooling and the rocky coves.

Parking: in summer, beach car parks fill up quickly. From the residence, the cycle paths take you to most beaches by bike.

Days off the beach: the residence's 2 heated pools are open from April to the end of September, weather permitting.
