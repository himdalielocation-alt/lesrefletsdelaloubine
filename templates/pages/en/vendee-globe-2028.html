Vendée Globe 2028: visiting Les Sables-d'Olonne for the start

The start of the Vendée Globe 2028, the solo, non-stop, unassisted round-the-world yacht race, will be given on Sunday 12 November 2028 at 1:02 pm off Les Sables-d'Olonne. It is the 11th edition of the race. Here is what you need to know to plan your trip, from the Les Reflets de la Loubine apartment, in the Lagrange L'Estran residence in La Pironnière.

The Vendée Globe village at Port Olona

Before each start, a race village is set up around the Port Olona basin for about three weeks: you can get close to the boats moored at the pontoons, meet the skippers, and enjoy events every day.

For the 2024 edition, the village was open from 19 October to 10 November, 10 am to 8 pm, over 30,000 m², and more than a million visitors were expected before the start. The 2028 village dates have not been announced yet: this page will be updated as soon as they are.

Watching the start on 12 November 2028

On the morning of the start, the boats leave the pontoons one by one and sail down the channel of Les Sables-d'Olonne, cheered on by crowds packed along the jetties on both the Les Sables and La Chaume sides. The start gun is then fired offshore at 1:02 pm.

Tip from regulars: arrive very early, as the best spots along the channel are taken in the first hours. Traffic and parking are very busy that day: cycling is a good option, with the cycle path starting right outside the residence.

The finishes, early 2029

The Vendée Globe ends where it began. After more than two months at sea for the fastest, the skippers sail back up the channel one by one from January 2029, often welcomed by large crowds, day or night. Another highlight, more intimate, for an off-season visit.

Staying at Les Reflets de la Loubine during the Vendée Globe

Our apartment for 4 people is on the ground floor of the Lagrange L'Estran residence, facing the Puits d'Enfer, with a direct sea view and free parking. It has a bedroom with 2 single beds and a wall bed in the main room, a kitchen with dishwasher, and is a few kilometres from the town centre and Port Olona.

In November the residence's pools are closed, but the wild coastline, walks to the Puits d'Enfer and the buzz of the race village will easily fill your days.
