How to get to Les Sables-d'Olonne

Our Les Reflets de la Loubine apartment is at 65 rue du Puits d'Enfer, Château-d'Olonne, 85180 Les Sables-d'Olonne, in the Lagrange L'Estran residence. Here is how to reach us by train, car or plane.

By train

Les Sables-d'Olonne station is linked to Paris-Montparnasse by direct TGV trains: allow about 3h30 for the fastest services, a little longer depending on the timetable. Other connections run via Nantes. Timetables vary with the seasons: check SNCF Connect.

The station is a few kilometres from the apartment: plan a taxi or the bus, or ask us for advice when you book.

By car

Les Sables-d'Olonne is reached via the A87 motorway to La Roche-sur-Yon, then the dual carriageway to the coast. From Nantes, allow about an hour and a half depending on traffic.

Once you arrive, you can use the residence's free parking: the car can stay parked for your whole stay.

By plane

The nearest airport is Nantes Atlantique, from where you can reach Les Sables-d'Olonne by hire car (about an hour and a half) or by combining a shuttle and the train.

Getting around

The nicest way is by bike: the cycle paths start right at the residence and follow the coast towards the town centre, Tanchet and the beaches. In summer it is often quicker than the car, as parking near the beaches is difficult.

On foot, you are a few minutes from the Puits d'Enfer and the wild coast. The town centre and the Grande Plage are about 4 km away.

Arrival and departure

Check-in from 5 pm, check-out by 10 am. We send you all the practical information once your booking is confirmed.
