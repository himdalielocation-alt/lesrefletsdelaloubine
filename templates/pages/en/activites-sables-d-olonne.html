Things to do in Les Sables-d'Olonne? Our activity ideas around La Pironnière

Planning a stay in Les Sables-d'Olonne and wondering how to fill your days? From the Les Reflets de la Loubine apartment, located in the Lagrange L'Estran residence in La Pironnière, everything is within easy reach: wild coves, the lively town centre, the harbour and the great Vendée cycle paths. Here is our tried-and-tested selection.

The beaches: from fine sand to secret coves

La Pironnière beach is the closest to the residence. It's family-friendly and sheltered, and our favourite early in the morning, when the tide uncovers the rocks. Children love hunting for crabs and shells there.

A little further on, the Cayola cove is a small inlet surrounded by rocks. It's one of the most photogenic spots on the coast, especially at sunset.

For a classic seaside-resort feel, head to the Grande Plage, in the heart of Les Sables. Its kilometres of fine sand and calm water make it a safe bet for families. Surfers and nature lovers will prefer Sauveterre beach, to the north, lined with dunes and forest.

The Puits d'Enfer and the coastal path

Just minutes from the residence, the Puits d'Enfer ("Hell's Well") is a spectacular rocky chasm where the sea crashes in dramatically during rough weather. The coastal footpath offers superb ocean views. It's a great walk in any season, including winter, when the waves are at their most impressive.

The Remblai and the town centre

You can't come to Les Sables without strolling along the Remblai, the famous promenade running along the Grande Plage. It's lined with cafés, ice cream parlours and restaurants with sea views.

Don't miss the Halles centrales, the covered market, to stock up on local produce: fresh-off-the-boat fish, sardines, fleur de sel, préfou bread and Vendée brioche. Right next door, the Île Penotte quarter will surprise you with its lanes decorated in seashell mosaics.

For rainy days, the Musée de l'Abbaye Sainte-Croix (MASC) has excellent modern and contemporary art collections.

La Chaume, the fishermen's quarter

On the other side of the channel, La Chaume has kept its soul as a fishing village, with low houses and colourful lanes. Cross over on le passeur, the small ferry that has linked the two banks for generations, then climb up to the Arundel tower and the Saint-Nicolas priory for a stunning view over the harbour entrance.

Port Olona, the sailing capital

Les Sables-d'Olonne is world-famous as the start and finish port of the Vendée Globe, the solo round-the-world yacht race. Even outside race season, a walk along the pontoons of Port Olona lets you admire dozens of sailing boats. You can also head out for a boat trip or a fishing outing from the harbour.

By bike: the Vélodyssée right from the residence

Good news for cyclists: the cycle paths start right outside the residence. Les Sables lies along the Vélodyssée, the great Atlantic coast cycle route. You can follow the coastline, ride through the Olonne forest in the shade of the pines, or head out to the salt marshes, where salt and fleur de sel are still harvested by hand.

With family: the zoo and Lake Tanchet

With children, the Zoo des Sables-d'Olonne is a must. This human-scale park, tucked away in greenery, can easily be visited in half a day. Nearby, Lake Tanchet is perfect for a quiet walk or a picnic.

And of course, in season, the residence's two heated pools (open May to October) let you swim in peace on days when the ocean is too cool.

The year's big events

Les Sables lives to the rhythm of sporting and sailing events that draw visitors from all over France:

  • the IRONMAN Les Sables-d'Olonne-Vendée, in late June, an impressive spectacle even for non-athletes;
  • spring races and regattas, such as the Vendée Va'a (Polynesian outrigger canoes) in May;
  • European Heritage Days in September;
  • the start of the Vendée Globe, scheduled for November 2028, which turns the town into a race village for three weeks.

Remember to book early if your stay falls during one of these highlights.
