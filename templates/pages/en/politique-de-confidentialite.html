Privacy Policy

What data is collected

This site has no contact form and no user account. There is no analytics cookie or tracking script. The only data we receive is what you send us yourself, via the "Check my dates" link (mailto button) or directly to himdalielocation@gmail.com: your name, your email address, and any information you choose to include in your message (desired stay dates, number of people, phone number if you provide it, etc.).

Why this data is used

Solely to communicate with you about your booking request and, where applicable, to organise your stay.

Retention period

To be added.

Who receives this data

No one other than the publisher of this site. Your data is never sold, shared with a third party, or used for any commercial purpose other than processing your request.

Third-party services used by the site

The location map (in the "Location" section) loads OpenStreetMap map tiles only when you scroll down to that section — never on initial page load. This triggers a request to OpenStreetMap's servers, subject to their own privacy policy (osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). No other external resource is loaded by this site.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, as well as a right to object. To exercise these rights, write to himdalielocation@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).
