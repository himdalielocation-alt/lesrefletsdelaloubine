Les Sables-d'Olonne off season: our ideas for autumn and winter

Far from the summer bustle, Les Sables-d'Olonne shows another face from November to April: a spectacular ocean, near-empty beaches, a slower pace… and gentler prices. Here are our ideas for an off-season stay from our Les Reflets de la Loubine apartment.

{{PHOTO:act-puits-enfer}}

The ocean show

Autumn and winter are when the Atlantic is at its most impressive. On spring-tide days or when the swell is up, head to the Puits d'Enfer, right opposite the apartment, or onto the coastal path along the corniche: the waves crash there in sprays of foam.

The Remblai, the promenade along the Grande Plage, is also lovely off season, especially in the low late-afternoon light.

Walking and cycling

The cycle paths start right outside the residence: in the low season you will almost have them to yourself. Follow the coast, ride through the Olonne forest or go as far as the salt marshes.

The beaches around La Pironnière are also perfect for long walks at low tide, when the sand stretches out and the beach is deserted.

Unwind: thalasso and wellness

Les Sables-d'Olonne has several thalassotherapy centres, one of them about 2 km from the apartment. Heated seawater pool, treatments, steam room: ideal after a windy walk. Remember to book, especially during school holidays.

Rainy days

Head into town: the Halles centrales covered market for seafood and local produce, the Abbaye Sainte-Croix museum (MASC) for modern and contemporary art, and the shopping streets of the centre.

Several attractions, such as the Zoo des Sables or the Aquarium de Vendée, are open for much of the year: check their opening hours before you go, as they vary with the seasons.

Off-season highlights

Every four years, autumn is marked by the Vendée Globe. The next start will be on 12 November 2028, with a race village open from 20 October to 12 November: everything you need to know to attend.

Why come in the low season

In the low season (November to April, outside school holidays), our apartment is available from €50 a night, with a 2-night minimum. For up to 4 people, with a sea view opposite the Puits d'Enfer and free parking.

The only catch: the residence's pools are open from April to the end of September, weather permitting. But the total peace and the ocean in all its moods more than make up for it.
